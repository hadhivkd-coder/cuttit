# Reels Kit: 10 Claude skills, 10 reels, 2 days

Each reel shows one result Claude made, ends with **"Comment CLAUDE and I'll send you the skill"**,
and a DM bot sends the free `SKILL.md`. Followers come from the free skills; money comes later from a paid pack.

## How someone uses a skill you send them
1. Put the folder in `~/.claude/skills/<skill-name>/SKILL.md` (Claude Code), or upload it as a skill in the Claude app.
2. Ask Claude something like "make me a city map reel of Kochi".

## The 10 reels

| # | Skill | Reel idea (hook in the first second) | Comment word |
|---|-------|--------------------------------------|--------------|
| 1 | city-map-reel | "I didn't touch a single keyframe" + map flight of YOUR city | MAP |
| 2 | word-captions | Same clip with vs without captions, side by side | CAPTIONS |
| 3 | kinetic-quote | A famous line animated to a beat in 30 s | QUOTE |
| 4 | bar-chart-race | "Biggest cities in India, 1950 to 2025" | DATA |
| 5 | photo-to-ad | 5 phone photos of a local shop -> a real ad | AD |
| 6 | carousel-maker | Swipe through a carousel Claude wrote and designed | SLIDES |
| 7 | before-after-reel | A room / photo transformation with a slider wipe | WIPE |
| 8 | itinerary-reel | "3 days in <your city>" with an animated map | TRIP |
| 9 | audiogram-clip | Best 40 s of a podcast, auto-picked | CLIP |
| 10 | website-scroll-reel | "Claude built this website in 20 minutes" | SITE |

Using a different word per reel tells you which skill people want most, so you know what to sell first.
If you want one word everywhere, use CLAUDE.

## Schedule
**Day 1:** post #1 at 9 am, #2 at 12 pm, #3 at 3 pm, #4 at 6 pm, #5 at 9 pm.
**Day 2:** post #6 to #10 at the same times.
Make all 10 reels before Day 1 starts, so posting day is only posting and replying.

## Reel formula (15-25 s)
1. **0-1 s hook:** the finished result, plus big text ("No After Effects. Just Claude.").
2. **1-15 s:** the result playing, with a 2-3 s glimpse of the prompt or the Claude screen.
3. **Last 2 s:** "Comment MAP for the free skill" on screen. Say it in the caption too.

## Caption template
```
I made this <thing> with Claude in <N> minutes - no editing software.

Want the exact skill I used? Comment <WORD> and I'll DM it to you free.

Follow @<you> - 10 free Claude skills this week.
#claudeai #aitools #reels #<city>
```

## Auto-DM on comments
Use a Meta-approved tool such as **ManyChat** (it has a free tier), linked to your Instagram professional account:
trigger = comment contains the word, action = DM with the link to the skill file (a Google Drive link or a Gumroad "$0+" product).
Ask people to follow before the DM link opens ("Follow + I'll send it"). Don't make following a hard requirement, which breaks Instagram's rules.

## Selling after you reach 500+ followers
- Free: one skill per reel, the way you've been giving them out.
- Paid pack (Gumroad, Payhip, or Lemon Squeezy): all 10 skills + a video walkthrough + updates, about Rs 499 / $9 to launch.
- Upsell: "I make the reel for you" as a done-for-you service for local businesses (photo-to-ad, website-scroll-reel).

## Be honest about it
- Make your own reels from these skills. Don't repost other creators' videos, prompts, or camera files.
- Google Earth renders need the "Google Earth" credit kept on screen; OpenStreetMap data needs "(c) OpenStreetMap contributors".
- Follower numbers aren't guaranteed. Watch which comment word wins and make more of that.
