# cuttit: ₹3,00,000 in 21 days

## 1. The honest maths
₹3L / 21 days = **₹14,300 a day**. At ₹99 per skill that is **3,030 sales**: about 1 lakh store visitors at a
typical 3% conversion. Not realistic from a new account. So the plan is **not** "sell more ₹99 skills".
It is: **use ₹99 to get buyers in, then sell them bigger things.** Average order value is the lever.

## 4. The systems

### A. Skill factory (1 new style every 2 days → 10 styles by Day 21)
Every style goes through the same pipeline, with a checklist:
reference video → frame/audio analysis → style DNA (`style.json`) + SKILL.md → test render on a demo clip →
before/after assets (landscape cover, 9:16 reel, side-by-side) → PDF guide (same template) → store listing →
content pack. The shared renderer means a new style is mostly new rules and a few new effects, not a rebuild.
Naming: one-word style names in one family (Byline, then e.g. Margin, Headline, Cinema...).

### B. Content engine (every style = 5 pieces of content)
1. **Before → After reel** (the hook: "I didn't edit this. Claude did.")
2. **"How" reel**: screen recording of Claude making the edit
3. **Carousel**: the style's moves, frame by frame
4. **Stories**: polls ("which style next?"), launch countdown
5. **Comment-to-DM**: keyword = style name → product link
Post **2-3 reels a day**. Every reel ends with the comment keyword.

### C. Funnel
Reel → comment keyword → ManyChat DM with the link → store (₹99 skill) → cart upsell: "add the 5-style pack for ₹499"
→ after purchase: join the WhatsApp community → All-Access and done-for-you offers → course in week 3.

### D. Ads (from Day 7, once one organic reel clearly converts)
- ₹1,000-1,500/day on Meta, promoting the **best organic reel**, sending to **All-Access / Style Pack**, not the ₹99 item.
- Createpie may not support a Meta Pixel. If not, run ads to our own landing page (built, with Pixel) linking to the store.
- Kill an ad after ₹500 with no sale; scale the winner.

### E. Partnerships
- 10-20 small creators/editors as **affiliates at 25-30%**: they post their own before/after.
- As a Createpie partner, ask for marketplace featuring.

### F. Proof log (feeds the course)
Every day: what Claude built, screenshots, numbers. This becomes the course and the most trusted content.

## 6. Daily scorecard
Reel views · comments on keywords · DMs sent · store visits · orders · average order value · revenue · ad spend.
Review every night; double down on the top reel and the top product.

## 7. Risks
- **Buyers need a computer + Claude Code.** Say it clearly; route phone-only people to done-for-you.
- **Copying:** skills are just files. Speed and the community are the moat, not secrecy.
- **Delivery:** check buyers can download the ZIP on a computer, not only in the Createpie phone app.
- **Done-for-you capacity:** each 60 fps reel renders in ~20+ min; cap orders per day.
