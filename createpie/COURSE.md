## Course description (long)
Edit reels by asking Claude in plain words.

This pack gives you 12 ready-made Claude skills. Each skill is a small instruction file you add to Claude once.
Then you ask for the reel ("make a map reel of my city", "add captions to this video") and Claude builds it for you:
it writes the code, runs the tools, and exports a 1080×1920 video ready to post.

**What's inside**
1. City map reel: a 3D flight along a real route through your city
2. Word captions: captions that pop word by word, synced to your voice
3. Kinetic quote: words that slam and slide in on the beat
4. Bar chart race: animated rankings from real data
5. Photos to ad: 5 phone photos turned into a 15-second product ad
6. Carousel maker: 7 to 10 designed slides, with the copy written for you
7. Before / after: slider wipe or split screen, with the two shots aligned
8. Itinerary reel: "3 days in your city" with an animated map
9. Podcast clips: the best moments picked out, with waveform and captions
10. Website + scroll: a one-page website plus a phone scroll video of it
11. Auto reframe: landscape video turned vertical, following whoever is speaking
12. Reel covers: covers and thumbnails, with the best frame picked for you

**What you need:** a Claude account. Claude Code (desktop app or VS Code) works best, because it can run the video tools on your computer. The setup guide in lesson 1 walks you through it.

**Includes:** all 12 skill files, the setup guide, and every skill released before 6 Dec 2026.
