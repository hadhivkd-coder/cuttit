What it does

Edits that make people ask “how did he do that?”

Everything in the demo was made by Claude with Byline, from one 10-second clip and four stock b-roll shots.

DEPTH

Text behind you

Giant words rise letter by letter behind your head and shoulders. You stay in front.

LINES

Orbit & swoosh lines

Glowing strokes draw themselves around you, behind your neck and across the frame, then retract.

B-ROLL

3D floating cards

B-roll flies in as tilted 3D cards over a blurred you, or opens with an iris reveal.

TYPE

Pop-in captions

1-2 word captions that pop and hard-cut, with big serif-italic power words.

GRAPHICS

Motion graphics on glass

Bar charts, padlocks, counters, calendars, icon chips: eased, floating, with a light shimmer.

FEEL

Flash transitions & sound

Soft white light flashes, smooth camera moves, and sound effects synced to every moment.

Not a template

Claude reads your script and designs the edit.

Byline finds the beats in what you say and picks the effect that fits each one, so no two videos look the same.

What you need

Honest requirements

  • A computer with Claude Code (desktop app or VS Code). It doesn't render on a phone.
  • Free tools: Python and ffmpeg. Claude installs the rest.
  • Render time: about 2 minutes per second of video at 60 fps.
  • Your own talking-head video, ideally 1080p. B-roll is optional.
Questions
Do I need editing experience?

No. You talk to Claude in plain language. Byline tells Claude exactly how to edit.

Can I use my own fonts, colours, music and sound effects?

Yes. Choose “customise” and Byline asks for them. Otherwise it uses its own defaults and generates its own sound effects and music bed.

Will every video look the same?

No. Byline reads your script and picks different effects for different beats, with one hero moment per video.

Can I use it for client work?

Yes. The reels you make are yours to post or sell. Please don't resell or share the skill files themselves.

Can I edit on my phone?

Not yet. Byline renders on a computer. Want it done for you? Our WhatsApp editing service is coming soon.
